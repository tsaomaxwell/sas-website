Privacy Policy

Starving Artist Society (SAS)

Starving Artist Society values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you interact with our platform, including artist submissions and newsletter sign-ups.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Portfolio links or social media handles
  • Artwork or creative submissions
  • Any additional information you choose to provide

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Review and evaluate artist submissions
  • Communicate with you regarding your submission
  • Share updates about features, events, or opportunities (if you’ve opted in)
  • Improve our platform and community experience

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

Your information may be shared with:

  • Internal team members for review purposes
  • Trusted third-party services (e.g., email platforms) solely to operate our services

4. Data Storage & Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights

You may:

  • Request access to your data
  • Request corrections or updates
  • Request deletion of your information
  • To do so, contact us at: strvingartistsociety@gmail.com

6. Third-Party Links

Our platform may include links to external websites (e.g., portfolios, social media). We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions, please contact us at: strvingartistsociety@gmail.com
